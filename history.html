HIS STORY.

David Goggins endured a childhood defined by relentless trauma, beginning in an abusive household where his father violently beat him, his brother, and his mother while forcing them to work long, unpaid nights at a family roller rink. After escaping to Indiana with his mother, Goggins faced extreme poverty, severe learning disabilities, and toxic stress that triggered a debilitating stutter, compounded by being one of the only Black students in a town rife with vicious racism and constant threats of violence. The compounding weight of unhealed trauma, the murder of his mother's fiancé, and crippling self-doubt eventually drove him into deep depression, leaving him working a dead-end job spraying for cockroaches and weighing nearly 300 pounds before he forged his pain into the legendary mental toughness he is known for today.
